# Complete raw upbow arms

The raw bow-mark pass previously used an approximate V fit and horizontal
coverage. A numeral with an interior crossbar or descending stem could satisfy
that fit and become an upbow.

Upbow admission now additionally partitions each physical row into literal
contiguous ink intervals. Both fitted arms must retain an eight-connected path
through every row to the tip. The two physical arms may merge into a thick or
rounded tip once; an internal crossbar cannot merge and reopen them. Every
reachable interval is retained, rather than choosing a favorable branch.

The original fit, raster tolerance, direction ambiguity refusal, owner/text
guards, and downbow admission remain unchanged. Only the raw bow-mark caller
uses this supplement; the shared head/shelter/fermata classifier is unchanged.
The additional component work and six row arrays are prepaid within the existing
page allowance. The proof reads no additional raw raster pixels.

`BowArmCompletenessTest` supplies thirteen original procedural scenes: complete
straight, slanted, rounded, uneven-top, shallow and thick bows, plus numeral,
spur, crossbar, branch, disconnected-row and italic-text controls. Three of
these expose failures before the repair. No commercial score raster or personal
data is included.

Recognition123 invalidates older derived interpretations. Guide299/audio101,
record layouts, model weights and dependencies are unchanged. Source/test
verification does not establish an installed app/worker deployment.
