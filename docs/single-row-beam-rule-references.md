# Single-row dark staff-rule references in beam ownership

A rasterized staff line can have one dark core row with lighter antialiasing on
either side. The local beam carrier classifier previously required at least two
dark rows for every reference. When a beam and a head obscure two other rules,
discarding a genuine one-row reference can leave fewer than the required three
references and turn the whole nominal staff band into unknown evidence.

The existing constructor and all two-or-more-row reference paths are unchanged.
The Source caller enables a paid additional case: a one-row core also needs a
unique closed thin raw run on both lateral flanks, at the established rule phase.
Absent, clipped, thick, rival or displaced flank ink refuses the reference. The
existing three-reference fit, phase, scale, contrast and mass comparisons still
apply. This supplies no pitch track, painted pixel, inferred missing rule or new
producer authority. Every added pixel read and scan operation spends the existing
Source logical-work allowance; exhaustion follows its existing whole-capture
refusal. Shaft, geometry and flag caps are unchanged.

One JUnit method runs102 original procedural assertions over four scales and
three raster phases, including missing/isolated/rival/thick/displaced references,
raw immutability, legacy behavior and exact/one-under budget boundaries. Existing
physical beam and whole-Source rivals are retained. These fields are original
Apache-2.0 examples; no commercial score pixels or private data are distributed.

Private saved-scalar Source qualification improves two complete visual-gold
groups, losing no old edge bits. That scope uses historical note/measure/staff
scalars and original rasters; it does not reconstruct unavailable producer clocks
or prove a fresh model/full native pipeline or packaged runtime. Cumulative
matched app/desktop-worker packaging remains deferred during the active audit.
